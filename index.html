Privacy Policy for UnknownProjectX using Google Game Service

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to UnknownProjectX ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of our users ("you"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application and related services, including features integrated with Google Play Games Services and Google OAuth.

2. Information We Collect and Receive

When you authenticate using your Google Account or utilize Google Services within UnknownProjectX, we may access or store the following information based on the permissions you explicitly grant:

  • Basic Profile Information: Your Google display name, profile picture URL, and public user ID.
  • Gaming Data: Game achievements, leaderboard scores, and player IDs generated via Google Play Games Services.
  • Google Account Email Address: Used solely to uniquely identify your game account and provide customer support.

We do not collect or access sensitive user data, financial details, contact lists, or precise location data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly to provide and improve the core functionality of UnknownProjectX. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Authenticate your identity and sign you into your game profile.
  • Save, sync, and display your game progress, achievements, and leaderboard ranks.
  • Provide developer support and process account-related requests.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, marketing profiling, commercial monetization, or sale to third parties.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information or Google user data with any third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

Your data is only processed locally on your device or transmitted securely to official Google Play Games Services servers to support leaderboards and cloud saves.

5. Data Retention and Security

  • Retention: We retain your account data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with game services.
  • Protection: We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption for all network traffic, to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

6. Data Deletion and Revoking Access

You have full control over your data and access permissions:

  • Revoking Permissions: You can revoke UnknownProjectX's access to your Google Account at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions Settings.
  • Data Deletion Requests: To permanently delete all account records and game save data associated with your profile from our systems, please send an email to unknownprojectx@outlook.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request." We will fulfill your request within 30 days.
